**Implementation** **&** **Interaction** **Considerations**

 • **Typography** **Implementation:** Load Aguila and Montserrat fonts
 via CSS using @font-face declarations or external services (e.g.,
Google Fonts for Montserrat). Ensure fallback fonts (e.g., sans‑serif)
are defined.
>
• **Colour** **Usage:** Use dark teal or black backgrounds for
high‑impact sections (hero, footer) and light Ivory backgrounds for
content sections to create contrast and maintain a luxurious feel.
Bronze and copper tones should be used sparingly as highlights.
>
 • **Animations:** Keep animations subtle. Scroll‑triggered text
 reveals and cross‑fades should last ~1.5–2 seconds to avoid
 overwhelming users. Use fade or slide transitions for product card
 hover effects.

 • **Responsive** **Design:** Ensure layouts adapt seamlessly across
 devices. For mobile, the product grid should become a single column;
 the hero text should stack; navigation becomes a hamburger menu. Keep
 the text legible with appropriate scaling.

 • **Accessibility:** Maintain high colour contrast; provide alt text
 for images; ensure keyboard navigation for interactive elements
(filter tabs, carousels, modals).
